About Coaching

This is a demo page showing how the AI appointment bot works on a typical coaching/consulting website. Click the chat bubble in the bottom right corner to interact with the bot!

🤖 Demo Mode: This bot is running locally. In production, it would be connected to a real calendar (Cal.com) and would book actual appointments.

💬 Natural Conversation

The AI engages visitors naturally, qualifying them and guiding them to book.

📅 Real-Time Booking

Connects to Cal.com to show real availability and book appointments.

🎨 Customizable

Colors, name, and personality can be customized for each client.

How It Works

The chat widget can be embedded on any website with just a few lines of code. When visitors interact with it, the AI:

  1. Greets them warmly
  2. Asks qualifying questions
  3. Shows available time slots
  4. Collects their info and books the appointment
  5. Sends confirmation
